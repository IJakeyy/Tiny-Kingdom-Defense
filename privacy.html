Privacy Policy

Tiny Kingdom Defense

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Tiny Kingdom Defense ("the game") is made by IsaJakeyy, an independent developer. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and who it is shared with. The short version: the game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer does not run any servers that collect your data.

1. Playing offline

Solo play, the tutorial and your settings work entirely on your computer. Your progress (which islands you have beaten and your best times), your achievements and your settings (volume, fullscreen, player name and hero) are saved in files on your own computer. Only your achievements are ever sent anywhere, and only while you are signed in (see below). If you turn on "Save match reports" in Settings, game summaries are also saved on your computer only. This option is off by default.

2. Playing online

Online play uses Epic Online Services, provided by Epic Games. When you choose Play Online, you can either play as a guest or sign in with your Epic Games account.

  • Guest: Epic creates an anonymous player ID linked to your device. No name, email or password is needed.
  • Sign in with Epic: with your permission, the game receives your Epic account ID and display name (Basic Profile), your Epic friends list (Friends) and your online status (Presence). The game never sees your email address or password.

While you play online, the game uses this information to:

  • show your display name (or the name you chose in the game) to the other players in your room;
  • create and find game rooms. A room stores its 5-letter code, the host's name, the game mode and whether it is open or listed in Quick Match;
  • show which of your Epic friends are playing, let you join their rooms and send them invites;
  • set your Epic status to "Playing Tiny Kingdom Defense";
  • post your Daily Challenge score to the online leaderboard. Epic stores your best score of each day together with your player ID, and the leaderboard shows your display name and score to other players.
  • record your achievements on Epic. While you are signed in (as a guest or with Epic), the game tells Epic which achievements you have unlocked; Epic stores them, with the time you unlocked them, together with your player ID. Achievements unlocked while offline are sent the next time you sign in.

During an online match, the computers of the players in the room connect to each other, either directly or through Epic's relay servers, to send game data such as unit orders. As with any online game, a direct connection can let other players' computers see your IP address.

3. Who your information is shared with

Only with Epic Games, which provides the online services, and with the other players in your online rooms, as described above. Epic Games handles this information under the Epic Games Privacy Policy. The game is downloaded from itch.io, which has its own privacy policy. The developer does not sell or rent your information and does not share it with anyone else.

4. Keeping and deleting your information

The developer does not keep a copy of your information. Rooms disappear when a match ends. To remove the game's access to your Epic account, go to your Epic Games account settings, open Apps and Accounts and remove Tiny Kingdom Defense. To delete your local progress and settings, delete the game's folder in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Tiny Kingdom Defense.

5. Children

The game does not ask for anyone's age, real name or contact details. Epic Games applies its own rules, including parental controls, to Epic accounts used by children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
